🧠 Behavioral Science
  • Raw Discount: Empathy deals were ₹525 cheaper (p = 0.0087, d = -0.56) in unadjusted comparisons.

🛒 Human Consumers
  • Dangers of Lowballs: Aggressive lowballs trigger defensive ask-freezes and lead to panic capitulation.
  • Collaborative Anchors: Moderate, polite offers extract the deepest concessions from algorithmic counterparties.
⚙️ AI System Architects
  • Hard Reservation Bands: Conversational LLMs must be bounded by programmatic price floors to prevent drift.
  • Consistency Guards: Automated validation checks prevent models from undercutting buyer bids during multi-turn chats.
