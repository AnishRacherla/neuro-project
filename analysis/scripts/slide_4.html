Study Design, Participant Pipeline & Validation

Two-arm randomized controlled trial (N = 101) with pre-registered screening and psychometric checks

1. Incentive Compatibility

Surplus-to-points tournament (1 pt per ₹10 saved). Top 3 won ₹1,500, ₹1,000, and ₹500 Amazon vouchers; impasses earned 0 pts.

2. Temporal Separation & Blinding

Pre-survey traits → Live bargaining → Post-survey appraisals. Neutral cover story; only ~5% guessed the true hypothesis.

3. Privacy by Design & IRB

Fictional roleplay personas, zero PII collection, zero-retention commercial API settings, and educational post-task debriefing.
