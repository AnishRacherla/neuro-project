Theoretical Foundations: Two Competing Views

Why should an AI agent care about tears or numbers?

1. Classical Game Theory

  • Rational Utility Maximization: A seller should discount an asset exclusively when presented with objective valuation data or credible alternatives (BATNA).
  • Emotional "Cheap Talk": Personal circumstances carry zero informational value and should be ignored by an intelligent agent.

2. RLHF Behavioral Hypothesis

  • Instruction Tuning & RLHF: Models undergo reinforcement learning heavily optimized for agreeableness, conflict avoidance, and helpfulness.
  • The Politeness Trap: Evaluators penalize models that appear cold or callous when humans express distress.
  • Our Prediction (H1): Human vulnerability exploits the LLM's RLHF agreeableness bias to extract deeper financial discounts.

Methodological Rigor: The 6 Behavioral Pillars

Guarding against common method bias and experimental artifacts

  • 1. Incentive Compatibility: Performance tournament converting buyer surplus into points (1 pt / ₹10 saved) with a walkaway penalty (0 pts).
  • 2. Single-Blind Protocol: Neutral cover story; participants blind to alternate conditions to prevent Hawthorne / demand effects.
  • 3. Temporal Separation: Pre-survey (traits) → Negotiation Chat → Post-survey (perceptions). Zero cognitive priming.
  • 4. Objective Dependent Variable: Final agreed price (₹) automatically extracted from server transaction logs—not self-reported.
  • 5. Privacy by Design: Scripted fictional roleplay, zero-retention commercial API terms, automated PII transcript scrubbing.
  • 6. Pre-Registered Screening: Transparent CONSORT exclusion pipeline filtering low-effort entries.

Behavioral Paradoxes & Corpus Linguistics

Surprising psychological phenomena emerging from human-machine bargaining

The 4 Behavioral Paradoxes

  • Anchoring Backfire: Economic lowballs (₹20,305) froze Max, forcing buyers to concede +₹5,755 upward. Empathy opened at ₹23,432 and conceded only +₹2,103.
  • Haggle Comfort Paradox (r = +0.378): Confident hagglers paid ₹26,334; timid hagglers paid ₹25,337 (a ₹1,000 advantage!). Human aggressive tactics backfired.
  • SVI Dunning-Kruger Effect: Economic buyers rated their own competence higher (5.81/7) than Empathy buyers (5.67/7) despite overpaying by ₹525.
  • Server Guard Events (+65%): Empathy triggered 65% more guardrail intercepts (0.33 vs. 0.20), proving the LLM was pushed to the brink of over-concession.

Corpus Text Analytics (1,007 Utterances)

  • Personal Need Words: 64.0% in Empathy vs. 7.1% in Economic (9.0x ratio, p < 0.0001).
  • Market/Spec Words: 69.3% in Economic vs. 9.6% in Empathy (7.2x ratio, p < 0.0001).
  • Seller Emotional Validation: Max validated buyer distress in 51.8% of Empathy turns vs. only 27.1% of Economic turns (χ2 = 28.7, p < 0.0001).

Ethical Safeguards & Privacy by Design

Adhering to strict human-subject IRB ethics and data minimization

Participant Protection

  • Scripted Fictional Personas: Participants instructed to adopt fictional roleplay scenarios; strictly forbidden from sharing real personal life trauma or medical records.
  • Data Minimization (GDPR): Omitted unnecessary demographics (gender, education); collected only age for 18+ verification and covariate control.
  • Automated Regex PII Scrubbing: Active server regex sanitized emails, phone numbers, and handles prior to LLM submission and database storage.

API Safety & Full Debriefing

  • Zero Data-Retention Commercial API: All dynamic model calls processed under commercial zero-retention terms (not stored or used to retrain models).
  • Full Post-Study Debriefing: Clarified study goals, revealed that Max was an AI seller, and confirmed that the transaction was entirely fictional.
  • Voluntary Consent: Cryptographic IDs with absolute right of withdrawal at any point.

Conclusions & Practical Implications

Key takeaways for consumers, AI engineers, and market governance

Core Scientific Takeaways

  • 1. Empathy Beats Economic Rationality: Human emotional vulnerability extracts ₹525 greater financial discounts (p = 0.0087) by activating RLHF agreeableness.
  • 2. Warmth Mediates Concessions: Empathy shifts conversational tone, which mechanically unlocks the LLM's price reductions (Sobel z = -2.48, p = 0.013).
  • 3. The AI Trust Paradox: Baseline machine trust acts as a tactical asset (β = -553.19), fostering reciprocal concession cycles.

Implications & Future Work

  • For Consumers: Polite, cooperative perspective-taking is the most cost-effective way to negotiate with conversational AI.
  • For AI Developers & Retailers: Autonomous commercial bots must be equipped with hardened programmatic price floors to prevent financial margin collapse when buyers exploit the LLM's empathy bias with sob stories.
  • Future Boundary Testing: Generalizability across enterprise models (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet) and high-stakes contractual domains.
