Core Research Question: When negotiating with an autonomous language model, does conversational empathy and human vulnerability elicit greater financial concessions than analytical market logic?

Theoretical Foundations & Competing Hypotheses

The clash between Classical Axiomatic Game Theory and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

📉 Classical Game Theory (Rationality)
  • Axiomatic Framework: Concessions occur strictly in response to verifiable market alternatives (BATNA) and hardware depreciation (Nash, 1950; Rubinstein, 1982).
  • Emotion = Cheap Talk: Personal stories carry zero economic signal and should be completely ignored by an intelligent seller.
  • Core Prediction: Analytical market facts extract price cuts; emotional pleas extract ₹0.
💚 RLHF Alignment Hypothesis (Emotion)
  • RLHF Agreeableness: Models are explicitly trained with human feedback to be polite, cooperative, and conflict-avoidant (Ouyang et al., 2022).
  • Vulnerability Penalty: Human evaluators penalize AI models that appear cold, rigid, or indifferent to human distress.
  • Core Prediction (H1): Human vulnerability triggers the LLM's helpfulness bias, yielding deeper discounts than factual logic.
The Scientific Empirical Test: Do autonomous LLMs behave like rational Nash bargainers (yielding strictly to market numbers), or do they inherit human-like prosocial emotional accommodation (yielding to vulnerability)?

Study Design, Participant Pipeline & Validation

Two-arm randomized controlled trial (N = 101) with pre-registered screening and psychometric checks

1. Incentive Compatibility

Surplus-to-points tournament (1 pt per ₹10 saved). Top 3 won ₹1,500, ₹1,000, and ₹500 Amazon vouchers; impasses earned 0 pts.

2. Temporal Separation & Blinding

Pre-survey traits → Live bargaining → Post-survey appraisals. Neutral cover story; only ~5% guessed the true hypothesis.

3. Privacy by Design & Ethics

Fictional roleplay personas, zero PII collection, zero-retention commercial API settings, and educational post-task debriefing.

Behavioral Dynamics & Computational Linguistics

Emergent behavioral paradoxes and text-level accommodation across 1,007 dialogue turns

Emergent Behavioral Paradoxes
1. Upward Concession Disparity:
Economic buyers opened at ₹24,476 and moved up +₹1,584 to settle. Empathy opened at ₹23,391 and moved up +₹2,144.
2. Haggle Comfort Paradox (r = +0.378):
Overconfident hagglers paid higher prices (₹26,334 vs. ₹25,337) because aggressive ultimatums triggered defensive ask-freezes.
3. Guard Interventions:
Empathy triggered 65% more programmatic guard interventions (0.33 vs. 0.20/session) where the LLM attempted to over-concede.

Research Ethics & Honest Limitations

How we ensured participant wellbeing, data privacy, and academic transparency

🛡️ Ethical Safeguards

1. Informed Digital Consent & Full Debrief

Pre-task explicit consent. Post-task educational debrief revealed the true hypothesis, AI nature of "Max", and fictional instructions.

2. Scripted Fictional Roles — Zero Real PII

Participants adopted fictional student/intern personas. Instructions strictly forbade sharing real personal details or private events.

3. Data Minimization & Zero-Retention API

No names, emails, or device IDs stored. Transcripts cleaned with regex PII scrubbers; commercial API ran in zero-retention mode.

⚠️ Observed Limitations

1. Bundled Strategy (Compound Treatment)

Empathy bundled lower initial offers, warm language, and student urgency together. Future work requires a 2×2 factorial design to isolate levers.

2. Sensitivity to Agreement Selection (ITT)

Significant in completed deals (p = 0.0087), but attenuates to marginal significance when impasses are factored in (p = 0.062).

3. Model & Domain Boundaries

Evaluated on Meta Llama-3 in peer-to-peer electronics. Corporate B2B procurement systems may enforce far tighter algorithmic constraints.

Conclusion & Strategic Implications

Evidence-based insights for behavioral researchers, human consumers, and AI system architects

🧠 Behavioral Science
  • Raw Discount: Empathy deals were ₹525 cheaper (p = 0.0087, d = -0.56) in unadjusted comparisons.
  • Control Absorption: Vanishes under multiple regression (+₹35, p = 0.92) once baseline trust and initial anchors are controlled.
🛒 Human Consumers
  • Dangers of Lowballs: Aggressive lowballs trigger defensive ask-freezes and lead to panic capitulation.
  • Collaborative Anchors: Moderate, polite offers extract the deepest concessions from algorithmic counterparties.
⚙️ AI System Architects
  • Hard Reservation Bands: Conversational LLMs must be bounded by programmatic price floors to prevent drift.
  • Consistency Guards: Automated validation checks prevent models from undercutting buyer bids during multi-turn chats.
